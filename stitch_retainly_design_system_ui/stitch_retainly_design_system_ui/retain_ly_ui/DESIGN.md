## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, developer-grade utility framework tailored for front-of-house food and beverage cashiers, counter operators, and multi-unit hospitality owners. It merges strict functional minimalism with refined editorial typography.

The personality balances high-velocity data capture with disciplined operational calm. It is utilitarian, razor-sharp, confident, and free of gratuitous decoration. Cashiers operate during intense rush periods; therefore, touch targets, field focus clarity, and immediate visual status scanning override ornamental flourishes. Store owners require clarity, high information density, and instant retention diagnostics across multiple service channels (dine-in, takeaway, aggregators, and direct messaging).

The visual language draws inspiration from modern developer toolkits and technical interfaces:
- **Canvas & Surface Hygiene:** A pure `#ffffff` canvas structured by hairline dividers (`#f0f0f3` and `#dcdee0`) and crisp graphite inking (`#171717`).
- **Typography as Architecture:** High-contrast headings set in Hanken Grotesk lend architectural structure and subtle mechanical authority, while Inter provides neutral, highly legible micro-copy and data points.
- **Pure Black Action Tokens:** Uncompromising `#000000` buttons serve as the absolute focal anchor for primary interactions, paired with high-voltage semantic accents for retention telemetry.

## Colors

The color palette reinforces operational hierarchy and rapid status recognition:

### Primary & Action Surfaces
- **Primary CTA (`cta-black` #000000):** Reserved exclusively for primary completion triggers such as "Simpan Order", "Masuk", and "Simpan Perubahan". Its active state shifts to `#1a1a1a`.
- **Primary Ink (`text-ink` / `primary_color_hex` #171717):** Delivers maximum text contrast on light surfaces for critical order fields, customer names, and active tabular states.

### Surfaces & Boundaries
- **Base Canvas (`canvas-base` #ffffff):** A crisp, pure white foundation for mobile and desktop screens.
- **Section Soft Surface (`canvas-soft` #fafafa):** Alternating surface for table headers, segment containers, and subtle view divisions.
- **Hairlines (`hairline-default` #f0f0f3, `hairline-strong` #dcdee0):** Define 1px structural boundaries. Component borders, cards, and resting input fields rely on `#dcdee0` to guarantee accessibility under ambient store lighting.

### Semantic Retention Hierarchy
Customer retention health relies on three color trios (text, surface background, and border):
- **Active (`semantic-active` #16a34a):** Denotes healthy repeat patterns. Accompanied by `#f0fdf4` surface fill and `#bbf7d0` border stroke for status tags.
- **At Risk (`semantic-risk` #ab6400 / #f59e0b):** Flags customers nearing churn thresholds. Paired with `#fffbeb` surface and `#fde68a` border.
- **Churned (`semantic-churned` #e11d48 / #eb8e90):** Highlights inactive patrons. Paired with `#fff1f2` surface and `#fecdd3` border.
- **Hyperlink & External Communication (`secondary_color_hex` #0d74ce):** Reserved strictly for actionable URL links, secondary metadata triggers, and WhatsApp outreach anchors.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines modern technical aesthetics with high data legibility:
- **Headlines (Hanken Grotesk):** Applied to screen headers, KPI metric titles, modal headers, and module groupings. The slight geometric tightening and negative tracking (-0.01em to -0.03em) give dashboards an engineered appearance without sacrificing readability.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** Serves operational touchpoints, table rows, form field values, and transaction records. Inter guarantees optical stability on compact mobile viewports and POS terminals.
- **Retention Data & Badges (`caption-uppercase`):** Status labels (e.g., `ACTIVE`, `AT RISK`, `CHURNED`, `5X ORDER`) use 11px uppercase Inter with `0.08em` tracking for quick visual scanning.
- **Numeric Telemetry (`numeric-stat`):** KPI summary metrics on the retention dashboard utilize 32px semi-bold Hanken Grotesk numerals with proportional spacing for direct numerical scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 4px/8px modular rhythm, supporting single-column mobile cashier operations as well as wide-viewport desktop management consoles:

### Layout Models
- **Mobile First PWA (< 768px):** Primary cashier mode. Operates as a single-column stack with `16px` lateral margins (`gutter-mobile`). Critical action bars (e.g., "Simpan Order", bulk contact downloads) remain sticky above a persistent `64px` bottom navigation container.
- **Desktop & POS Terminals (>= 768px):** The bottom bar detaches and mounts as a permanent `240px` left-rail sidebar containing store status, branch switcher, and vertical navigation items. Order entry forms and customer inspector views center inside a `form-max-width: 540px` focus column or deploy across a 12-column analytical grid bounded by `container-max-width: 1200px`.

### Touch Ergonomics & Spatial Rhythm
- **Cashier Hit Targets:** All mobile tap targets (order inputs, channel selection pills, and customer autocomplete dropdown suggestions) maintain a minimum vertical height of `44px` to `48px` with at least `8px` between adjacent interactive elements.
- **Data Densities:** Lists, table rows, and customer cards use compact `12px` to `16px` padding bounds, keeping order volume visible without vertical scrolling.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, relying instead on structural outlines, hairline dividers, and tonal contrast:

- **Resting Layer (Flat Hairlines):** Base cards, data tables, and input containers sit at elevation 0, bordered by a single `1px solid #dcdee0` stroke against the `#ffffff` canvas or `#fafafa` background band.
- **Interactive Depth (Card Hover):** Active cards trigger a subtle elevation shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`, providing smooth visual feedback on desktop pointers without muddying the interface.
- **Modal & Autocomplete Dropdown Elevation:** Floating customer suggestion dropdowns, confirmation sheets, and filter popovers use high-definition architectural elevation: `box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)` with a perimeter `1px solid #dcdee0` hairline.
- **Inverted Dark Tiers (Code / System Surfaces):** Specific operational metrics and telemetry blocks make use of `#171717` dark surface backdrops with `#ffffff` and `#b0b4ba` copy to establish sharp focal contrast against pure white cards.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a strictly calibrated corner radius hierarchy:

- **Interactive Controls (`8px` / `rounded-md`):** Primary and secondary buttons, form input fields, segmented tabs, channel dropdown selects, and icon boundary plates utilize a crisp `8px` radius. This maintains structural precision and visual harmony with developer-grade tools.
- **Containers & Surfaces (`12px` / `rounded-lg`):** Customer summary cards, order detail modules, settings partitions, and KPI cards strictly employ a `12px` corner radius.
- **Status Pills & Chips (`9999px` / `rounded-full`):** Reserved exclusively for metadata tags: `Active`, `At Risk`, `Churned`, order frequency counters (`5x order`), and channel chips (`Gofood`, `Dine-in`).
- **Strict Prohibition:** Full pill radiuses (`9999px`) must never be applied to primary CTA buttons or input fields.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (`button-primary`):** Full-bleed on mobile form footers. Background: `#000000`; text: `#ffffff`; border-radius: `8px`; min-height: `44px` (cashier mobile standard: `48px`); font: `Inter`, 14px, 500. Active/pressed: `#1a1a1a`.
- **Secondary Action (`button-secondary`):** Background: `#ffffff`; text: `#171717`; border: `1px solid #dcdee0`; border-radius: `8px`; min-height: `40px`. Hover: background `#f0f0f3`.
- **Utility / WhatsApp Action:** Background: `#16a34a`; text: `#ffffff`; border-radius: `8px`; height: `36px` to `40px`. Used for one-tap WhatsApp template triggers.

### 2. Form Inputs & Autocomplete
- **Order Input Field:** Background: `#ffffff`; border: `1px solid #dcdee0`; border-radius: `8px`; height: `44px`; padding: `12px 16px`; font-size: `15px`.
- **Focus State:** Border expands to `2px solid #171717` with zero offset blur rings.
- **Autocomplete Dropdown Panel:** Fixed below the active customer lookup field. Background: `#ffffff`; border: `1px solid #dcdee0`; border-radius: `8px`; box-shadow: `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
- **Suggestion Item:** Minimum height `48px`; displays customer name in bold (`14px`), phone number formatted as `08xx-xxxx-xxxx` in muted `#60646c`, and order history badge aligned right.

### 3. Retention Badges & Chips
- **Status Indicator Pill:** Height `22px`; padding: `2px 8px`; border-radius: `9999px`; text-transform: `uppercase`; font-size: `11px`; font-weight: `600`.
  - *Active:* Text `#16a34a`, background `#f0fdf4`, border `1px solid #bbf7d0`.
  - *At Risk:* Text `#ab6400`, background `#fffbeb`, border `1px solid #fde68a`.
  - *Churned:* Text `#e11d48`, background `#fff1f2`, border `1px solid #fecdd3`.
- **Order Count Badge:** Text `#171717`, background `#f0f0f3`, border `1px solid #dcdee0`.

### 4. Customer Cards & Daily Send Checklist Rows
- **Card Container:** Background: `#ffffff`; border: `1px solid #dcdee0`; border-radius: `12px`; padding: `16px`.
- **Follow-up Checklist Row:** Padding: `12px 16px`; border-bottom: `1px solid #f0f0f3`; display: flex; align-items: center; justify-content: space-between.
- **Completed State:** Checkbox selected triggers an immediate opacity reduction to `0.45` and draws a subtle strike-through across the customer name, confirming completion during end-of-day store routines.

### 5. Responsive Navigation
- **Mobile Bottom Navigation:** Height: `64px`; background: `#ffffff`; border-top: `1px solid #f0f0f3`; 4 to 5 icons with 11px labels (Home, Customer, Dashboard, Follow-up, Settings). Active item is highlighted with `#000000` icon/text fill; inactive items use `#999999`.
- **Desktop Sidebar:** Width: `240px`; background: `#ffffff`; border-right: `1px solid #f0f0f3`; displays branch title, branch code badge, and vertical navigation list with `8px` rounded interactive selection states.
